# Align directional eyes with actual Idle

2026-09-06 follow-up. The user's reference is the implemented Idle face: its
screen-right eye is slightly larger. The previous directional depth sign made
the screen-right eye substantially smaller when looking right, reversing that
reference. This update corrects the ordering and reduces the exaggeration.

## Implementation

- Yaw depth is `1 + side * facingX * 0.03`, so right-facing poses slightly enlarge
  the right eye and left-facing poses mirror them. Earlier depth was an inverse
  0.22 coefficient.
- Directional slant is `facingX * 0.40`; at the explicit UpRight x component 0.85,
  this gives the same 0.34 major-axis slope as Idle.
- Pair compression is reduced from 0.16 to 0.08, and pitch openness scaling from
  0.10 to 0.04, keeping directional proportions close to Idle.
- Eye-group translations, the 0.16-radius downward extent, eased direction/tap
  overrides, all enum values, and the allocation-free rendering contract remain
  unchanged. No app, serial, platform or Core2 behavior changes are included.

## Native comparison and acceptance

The focused real-C++ host renderer passes all existing checks, including all
1,040 combinations, nine-direction reflection/position, transition continuity,
RGB565 coverage, Thinking travel and Asleep continuity for all body styles.
New checks compare actual Idle (Mood Idle, Expression Auto, Gaze Auto) with
UpRight and UpLeft after deterministic settling, motion amount zero and delayed
blinks, rather than comparing a hand-drawn reference or substituted eye glyph.

At 200×200, measured left/right opaque eye extents are:

| Pose | Width | Height | Opaque eye pixels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idle | 14 / 15 | 28 / 30 | 205 / 237 |
| UpRight | 15 / 16 | 29 / 30 | 222 / 243 |

Both have the intended slightly larger right eye; UpLeft reverses the ordering.
Per-eye heights remain within 12% of Idle, width/height aspect differs by less
than 0.08, pair spacing by less than 12%, and right/left ink ratio by less than
0.12. These tolerances account for native RGB565 pixel quantization. Existing
left/right reflected ink masks remain within their 12% difference bound, and
Down at 200px retains its 110–114px eye-center window.

The root reviewer visually accepted the actual three-pose native comparison
(Idle, UpRight, UpLeft) and nine-direction grid. Durable images are
`lib/bot-ux/docs/idle-up-right-raster.png` and `nine-directions-raster.png`.
The existing catalog's native examples and embedded direction appendix were
regenerated through its existing packager, without adding features or changing
its 31-entry count. Auto-mode GIFs do not use directional perspective, so their
regenerated bytes remain unchanged. This is host raster evidence; final Watch
firmware integration and native contact tracing are owned by the Watch agent.
